# Recovery ownership and cleanup inventory

Flux, Omni, OCI Ansible, TrueNAS native applications and their independent storage/backup mechanisms each retain their established recovery ownership. A snapshot, replicated volume, encrypted copy or successful backup operation is not proof of independently tested restoration. Consult private operator inventory before destructive changes.

The exact runbook, machine/security identifiers, operator evidence and
recovery prerequisites are retained in the
[private owner document](https://github.com/AgenticHomelab/homelab-secrets/blob/main/docs/recovery-ownership-inventory.md).

All production operations still require their separately approved changes,
current source review and verified live acceptance.
